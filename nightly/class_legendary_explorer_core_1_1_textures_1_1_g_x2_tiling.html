Detailed Description

Converts Wii U (GX2 / AMD R7xx AddrLib) tiled texture data to linear data.

ME3 WiiU stores each mip as an independently tiled surface with a swizzle of 0. Mips are 2D_TILED_THIN1 when they fill at least one macro tile, otherwise 1D_TILED_THIN1. Only the thin tile modes used by the WiiU cooker are implemented.
